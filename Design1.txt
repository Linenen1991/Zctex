Design changes:
Added Col4 "LogInformation" to LogListView.

And Start Match button have new requirement:
After click.
the 3rd column of SearchRuleTable contain Prefix+FixedString+Suffix
And it will take the rule to match the LogInformation
There is sample:
Case 1:
Prefix="123", Suffix="ABC", FixedCandidate="User"
LogInformation="There is no 123Replaced TextABC, And blahblah"
It will search the "123" and "ABC" and replace the text between the prefix and suffix
So new LogInformation will be:
"There is no 123UserABC, And blahblah"
